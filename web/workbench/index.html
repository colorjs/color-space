Workbench

Three directions for the color-space.io front page, built on one shared catalog and details pane – prototypes to choose between; the live atlas stays as it is.

Directions

  • Atlas

    Catalog-first, editorial

    The families as the page itself: each opens with its featured spaces at full reading, the rest follow as compact sliders, and the section titles are the navigation.

  • Guide

    Learning-first, then the atlas by purpose

    Starts by answering what a color space is and walks the start-here tour; the catalog follows, shelved by what each space is for.

  • Studio

    Tool-first, with a persistent inspector

    A working surface: the catalog beside an inspector that always holds a space – its channels, LUT, code and share – while the color stays in your hands.
